ACTUAL LICENSE TEXTS AND PROVENANCE
THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt contains exact upstream legal texts grouped by their SHA-256, including all relevant license files obtained for the 318 Windows crates, the 78 installed npm packages where supplied, Microsoft SDK notices, and Rust standard-library attribution. The JSON inventory identifies each component, version, source URL, source/lock checksum where available, original license path, text SHA-256, and text section. Public upstream author names in legal notices are retained attribution, not project ownership claims.

Where an explicit OR expression offers MIT, MIT is the preferred permissive alternative; the original alternative texts are preserved. AND expressions remain cumulative. Legacy slash expressions are retained verbatim and do not erase per-file notices. No LGPL alternative is selected where MIT or Apache alternatives exist. Mixed-license components include ring (Apache/ISC and embedded BoringSSL, once_cell, and fiat notices), brotli (BSD and MIT), dpi (Apache plus libm MIT), Unicode data, and webpki-roots CDLA-Permissive-2.0. The test-image license in image and the inactive SQLCipher license are excluded from the binary-preparation collection because those resources/features are not selected.
